With overflow: visible the content extends beyond the container and is visible. This can disrupt the design if not controlled.
In this example, a container with a fixed width and height contains a paragraph of text that is too long to display without scrolling. The overflow: auto; property causes scroll bars to appear in the container if the entire content cannot be displayed at once, allowing the user to scroll through it to view it.
